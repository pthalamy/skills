# Where to search: repositories, portals and access routes

Who holds each record and how to reach it. Names and URLs change; when a
link fails, search the repository's name plus the record type. Always
open the image behind an index hit: indexes are derivative and error-prone.

## Archives départementales

One per département (AD), each with its own website and viewer. All have
put online the état civil and parish registers (greffe collection),
tables décennales, most censuses, military registers (1867 to about
1921), the cadastre napoléonien, and often enregistrement tables,
hypothèques indexes, and notarial répertoires. Search: the AD's own site
(often "archives en ligne" or "état civil"), then FranceArchives
(francearchives.gouv.fr) which aggregates finding aids nationally.

Series to know (classification before 1940):

| Series | Content |
| ------ | ------- |
| B | Courts before 1790: tutelles, inventaires, sentences |
| C | Provincial administration; contrôle des actes 1693-1791 (2 C) |
| E | Feudal and family papers; notarial minutes (3 E); état civil and parish registers (E dépôt, 4 E, 5 E depending on the AD) |
| G | Secular clergy: diocesan archives, dispensations, parish accounts |
| H | Regular clergy: abbeys, hospitals (H dépôt) |
| L | Revolutionary period 1790-1800 |
| M | Administration after 1800: censuses (6 M), passports, livrets, police |
| P | Finance: cadastre (3 P), inscription maritime |
| Q | Domaines and enregistrement (3 Q), hypothèques (4 Q) |
| R | Military: conscription, matricules (1 R) |
| U | Courts after 1800 |
| X | Assistance: hospices, enfants assistés |
| Y | Prisons |

Ask the AD for the *état des fonds* or use FranceArchives to see what
exists for a commune; not everything is online, and what is online is not
always indexed.

## Communal and intercommunal archives

Communes keep their own copy of the état civil and parish registers
(collection communale), censuses, electoral lists, cemetery concessions,
délibérations. Large cities have their own archives (Paris, Lyon,
Marseille, Bordeaux, Nantes, Toulouse…) with online état civil. Small
communes: write to the mairie; recent acts (under 75 years) can be
requested by descendants with proof of filiation.

## Archives nationales and national bases

- **Minutier central des notaires de Paris** (AN, series MC): Paris
  notarial minutes; répertoires and some minutes online (siv.archives-
  nationales.culture.gouv.fr; ARNO indexes for 1551, 1751, 1761, 1851).
- **Reconstitution de l'état civil de Paris** (AN and Archives de Paris):
  pre-1860 acts partly reconstructed; fichier online at Archives de Paris.
- **Base Léonore**: Légion d'honneur dossiers with birth acts.
- **Naturalisations**: BB/11 dossiers, index in the *Bulletin des lois*
  and online (naturalisations 1814-1930 database).
- **Grand Mémorial / Mémoire des hommes** (Ministry of Defence): soldiers
  who died 1914-1918, military registers by name across départements,
  Indochina and Algeria, aviation, Seconde Guerre mondiale.
- **Base des enfants abandonnés**, **bagnards** (ANOM, IREL).
- **Archives de la Préfecture de police** (Paris): registers, dossiers.

## Military

- Registres matricules: AD series R, most online 1867-1921; class = birth
  year + 20; find the number in the alphabetical table of the class, then
  the register, at the recruitment office of the residence at 20.
- Before 1867: tirage au sort and conscription lists (AD R), contrôles de
  troupes 18th century (Service historique de la Défense, Vincennes,
  series Y), pensions dossiers.
- Officers and career soldiers: SHD Vincennes (dossiers individuels).
- Navy: inscription maritime (AD series P and SHD ports).
- Morts pour la France 1914-1918, 1939-1945: Mémoire des hommes; monuments
  aux morts (MémorialGenWeb).

## Diocesan and religious archives

Dispensations of consanguinity and affinity, banns dispensations,
ordinations, parish visitation records, registres de catholicité after
1792 (baptisms, marriages, burials recorded by the parish, precious where
civil registers are lost). Held at the évêché's archives or deposited at
the AD (series G, or a modern deposit). Access by appointment; some
dioceses have indexed dispensations online or via associations.

## Notarial records

Minutes are deposited at the AD (series 3 E, or E notaires) after 75
years, by étude. Find the notary through: the marriage act's mention of the
contract (from 1850), the enregistrement tables (tables des contrats de
mariage, des testaments, des successions et absences; from 1791) or the
contrôle des actes (1693-1791) which give date, notary and parties; the
notary's own répertoire (chronological list of acts). Without an
enregistrement entry, browse the répertoires of the notaries of the
nearest market town for the years around the event.

## Portals, indexes and trees

| Portal | What | Caution |
| ------ | ---- | ------- |
| Geneanet | Indexes, user trees, cemetery photos, relevés of associations, library | Trees are leads; check the underlying image |
| Filae | Nominal indexes of état civil and censuses for many départements (subscription) | Index errors; open the image |
| FamilySearch | Catalog by place (microfilms, digitised), indexes, trees; free | Catalog is the key: search the commune name |
| FranceArchives | National finding-aid aggregator | Leads to AD holdings, not always digitised |
| Bigenet, Généabank, GeneaBank | Association relevés (systematic transcriptions of BMS) | Access via membership or points |
| Cercles généalogiques départementaux | Relevés, help, journals | Often the only index for pre-1792 registers |
| MémorialGenWeb, Grand Mémorial | Soldiers, monuments | |
| Cimetières de France, Landrucimetieres, Geneanet cemeteries | Tombstones | Dates on stones are derivative |
| Gallica, RetroNews | Press, annuaires, faire-part, bulletins de lois | Full text OCR is imperfect; try variants |
| Wikimanche, Geneawiki | Commune histories, register availability | |

A hit in a user tree is a lead with `QUAY 0` or `1`; the plan must
convert it into a record.

## Libraries and press

Gallica (Bibliothèque nationale de France) for annuaires du commerce,
almanachs, bulletins de lois (naturalisations, name changes), local
newspapers (faire-part, faits divers, listes de conscrits). RetroNews for
press full text. Municipal libraries for local monographs and printed
genealogies. Local history societies' bulletins for relevés and studies of
a village's families.

## Abroad and colonial

- **ANOM** (Aix-en-Provence): état civil of former colonies (IREL online),
  bagnards, colonists.
- **Archives diplomatiques** (Nantes): état civil consulaire of French
  citizens abroad; acts also transcribed at the Service central d'état
  civil (Nantes) for post-1940 events.
- Neighbouring countries: Belgian State Archives (search.arch.be), Dutch
  Open Archives and WieWasWie, Italian Antenati (Portale Antenati,
  state archives' civil records), Spanish PARES, German Archion and
  Matricula (church registers), Swiss cantonal archives, Quebec BAnQ and
  Généalogie Québec, UK GRO and county record offices, US FamilySearch
  and state archives.

## How to ask an archive

Write in the archive's language with: the exact act (type, date or years,
commune or parish), the names as spelled, what you already checked and
where, and whether you want a scan, a certified copy or a reading-room
appointment. Cite the series or cote if FranceArchives gives one. Expect
weeks; log the request as a search with status pending.

## Recording a repository in a citation

For the `REPO` record and the `PAGE` of a citation: the archive's full
name and place; the series and cote; the register's title and year range;
the image or folio number as the viewer shows it; the permalink (ARK or
stable URL) when offered; the access date. A citation that works only
inside one website's session is not a citation.
